## gemmi

`src/spacegroups.rs` is generated by `tools/generate_spacegroups.py` from
gemmi's table of space-group settings: the number, Hermann-Mauguin symbol,
Hall symbol and symmetry operations of each of its 564 settings. The
generator also gives each setting its symbol as the International Tables now
write it, with the e glide of five groups where gemmi writes an older glide.
gemmi (<https://github.com/project-gemmi/gemmi>) is licensed under the
Mozilla Public License 2.0 (<https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/>). The
generator runs against an installed gemmi and is not part of the published
packages.

The generated table and its generator are available in the
[cifmi release tags](https://github.com/jcwang587/cifmi/tags). Select the tag
`v<package version>` and see `src/spacegroups.rs` and
`tools/generate_spacegroups.py` for the source corresponding to a release.

## Crystallography Open Database

The CIF files under `tests/data/cif/` are entries of the Crystallography Open
Database (<https://www.crystallography.net/cod/>), which places its data in
the public domain. Each file keeps its COD number in its name and its
bibliographic reference in its header; [tests/README.md](tests/README.md)
says what each one tests.
